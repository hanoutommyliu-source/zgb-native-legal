ZGB Native Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

ZGB Native, also known as Zoom Game Broadcaster, is a macOS app for preparing timed Zoom breakout-room broadcast messages and sending them from a queue during a Zoom meeting. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the App Uses

ZGB Native may use the following information to provide its meeting workflow:

  • Zoom authorization information needed to join meetings and use permitted Zoom Meeting SDK features.
  • Meeting details entered by the user, such as meeting number and passcode.
  • Broadcast message text, queue timing, saved session plans, and recent broadcast records created by the user.
  • Local app configuration, such as selected Zoom connection settings.

How Information Is Used

ZGB Native uses this information to authorize with Zoom, join a user-specified Zoom meeting, prepare broadcast queues, send host-controlled breakout-room broadcast messages, store reusable session plans, and show recent broadcast history in the local app.

Local Storage

ZGB Native stores queue content, saved session plans, recent broadcast records, and app configuration locally on the user's Mac. If OAuth backend mode is used, Zoom OAuth tokens may be stored locally by the backend so the app can request Zoom credentials and join meetings. The app does not intentionally include OAuth tokens, client secrets, meeting passcodes, or private credentials in app packages, source control, support messages, or Marketplace comments.

Sharing and Disclosure

ZGB Native sends meeting-related requests to Zoom as needed to authorize the app, join meetings, and perform user-directed meeting actions. Broadcast message text is sent to Zoom meeting participants only when the user chooses to send messages through the app. ZGB Native does not sell personal information.

Retention

Local queues, saved plans, recent broadcast records, configuration, and locally stored OAuth tokens remain on the user's Mac until removed by the user or replaced by normal app use. Deauthorizing the app in Zoom prevents future Zoom access for that account, but it does not automatically delete local files already stored on the user's Mac.

User Choices and Data Subject Rights

Users may remove the app from their Zoom account through the Zoom App Marketplace, delete the local macOS app, and delete local app/backend data stored on their Mac. Users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability for personal information handled by the developer by using the developer contact email listed on the Zoom Marketplace app profile. Available rights depend on applicable law and the user's location.

Security

ZGB Native is designed to keep app data local where practical and to use Zoom authorization for Zoom meeting access. Users should not share meeting passcodes, OAuth tokens, client secrets, or private credentials in support tickets or public comments.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ZGB Native changes. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact email listed on the Zoom Marketplace app profile.

ZGB Native is designed for use with Zoom meetings by users who have the required meeting permissions. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Zoom Video Communications, Inc.
