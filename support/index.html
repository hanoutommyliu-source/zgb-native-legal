ZGB Native Support

Last updated: June 2, 2026

ZGB Native, also known as Zoom Game Broadcaster, is supported by the ZGB Native app team for installation, Zoom authorization, meeting join, queue setup, and breakout-room broadcast workflow issues.

Contact Support

  • Email: tommy@halio.org
  • Support case link: create a support request by email
  • Support hours: Monday through Friday, 09:00-17:00 Bangkok time, excluding local public holidays.
  • First response target: within two business days.

Knowledge Base

The primary user guide is the ZGB Native Documentation. It includes installation, authorization, usage, troubleshooting, removal, and data-handling guidance.

Before Contacting Support

  1. Confirm that your Mac is running macOS 14.0 or later.
  2. Confirm that you downloaded the current review build from the official ZGB Native release link.
  3. Confirm that your Zoom account has authorized the ZGB Native Marketplace app.
  4. Confirm that the Zoom meeting number and passcode are correct.
  5. Confirm that the signed-in Zoom user has host, co-host, administrator, or equivalent permission for the meeting action being attempted.

What to Include

  • Your macOS version.
  • The ZGB Native app version shown in Finder or the app's About panel.
  • A short description of the workflow you were trying to complete.
  • The visible error message, if any.
  • Whether the issue happens during install, Zoom authorization, meeting join, queue setup, or breakout-room broadcast delivery.

Do not send Zoom meeting passcodes, OAuth tokens, client secrets, private credentials, or participant personal information in support requests.
